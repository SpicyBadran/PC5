Note on the three `$ awk …` lines above: they are shown with the shell's own escaping
doubled by the generator's here-document, so the printed form is not literally
copy-pastable. The command actually executed, and the one to use, is

    awk 'NF==3 && length($3)==64 {print $3"  "$1}' <manifest> | sha256sum -c -

run from /home/user/pc5. `length($3)==64` instead of `/^[0-9a-f]{64}$/` because Debian's
mawk does not enable interval expressions, and a recipe that selects nothing looks
exactly like a manifest with nothing wrong in it.
